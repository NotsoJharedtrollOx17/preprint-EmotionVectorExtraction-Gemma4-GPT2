\section{Results}

We evaluated the extracted emotion vectors utilizing (1) \texttt{openai-community/gpt-2-medium} and
(2) \texttt{google/gemma-4-e2b}, in which we performed four analyses: (i) Logit Lens, (ii) Emotion Steering, 
(iii) PCA Projection, and (iv) Cosine Similarity Heatmap. Each analysis was bounded by using the 9-emotions dataset, and 
the 20-emotions dataset respectively.

\subsection{GPT 2 Medium}

\subsubsection{Logit Lens}

Applying the Logit lens to the extracted emotion vectors in \texttt{openai-community/gpt-2-medium} produced token
distributions aligning consistently with their corresponding emotional labels. The highest-scoring tokens for each 
emotion reflected coherent affective language. For example, vectors associated with \texttt{``happy''} and 
\texttt{``sad''} yielded tokens corresponding to positive and negative emotional expressions related to their respective
emotion concept.


The resulting token sets were relatively concentrated, with limited inclusion of unrelated terms. This indicates that
the projected logits from the emotion vectors produce stable and interpretable outputs. In addition, this effect appears
for the list of 9 emotions, and 20 emotions as well, which can be appreciated in \Cref{tab:gpt2_logitlens_9,tab:gpt2_logitlens_20}.
